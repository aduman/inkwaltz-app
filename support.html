A little moving print.

Support

Happy to help. Email membrain.app@gmail.com and include InkWaltz in the subject. For a technical question, include your device, iOS version and app version from Settings. Please do not send private videos or payment details.

Make your first moving print

Choose a video or try the sample on Home. Pick a press, adjust its ink and paper, then open export review to save or share a section up to 30 seconds.

Saving your movie

Keep InkWaltz open while rendering. If rendering stops when you leave the app, your edit remains available so you can try again.

If Photos access is denied, allow InkWaltz to add videos in system Settings, or share the finished movie to Files. A failed Photos save can be retried without rendering again.

Restore InkWaltz Plus

Use the Apple Account you purchased with, then choose Restore Purchases in Settings. Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If the App Store is unavailable, check your connection and try again.

Manage your drafts

Use a draft’s menu on Home to rename or delete it. If a source video is missing, open the draft and choose its replacement when prompted. Remove imported or personal looks from All presses; existing drafts keep their own recipe copies.
